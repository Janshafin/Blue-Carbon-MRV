Methodologies and scoring rules governing submission eligibility.

AR-ACM0003 — Coastal Blue Carbon ACTIVE

Sentinel-2 Multi-Spectral · 10m Spatial Resolution

Afforestation/reforestation methodology applied to coastal mangrove restoration. Vegetation change is assessed via NDVI comparison between a claimed planting-date baseline and a recent monitoring window.

  • Eligibility thresholdScore ≥ 75, confidence band "high", zero flags
  • NDVI formula(B08 − B04) / (B08 + B04)
  • Monitoring windowRecent 30-day Sentinel-2 window
  • Photo GPS mismatch>1km from claimed location → −25
  • Photo timestamp mismatch>45 days from planting date → −15
  • Low current vegetationCapped score of 20
